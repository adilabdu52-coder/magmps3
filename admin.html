Backup

A copy of everything, in one file

Download

Every branch, every sale, staff, tanks, prices, credit customers, expenses, shifts, attendance, deliveries and corrections — the whole database, not the last few hundred rows. Not filtered by the branch selected above. Keep a copy somewhere other than the phone.

This is a record, not a restore button. Putting it back means inserting the rows in order and recreating sign-ins in Supabase first — logins are not in the file and cannot be. What it is good for: proving what the books said on a given day, moving to another project, and answering “what did we sell last March” after something has gone wrong.
